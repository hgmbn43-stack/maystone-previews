Common questions

Do you take the garden waste away?

Garden waste can be taken away as part of the job. Anyone carrying waste for you should have a waste carrier licence, which you can check on the Environment Agency register.

When is the best time to lay turf?

Autumn and spring are best, when the ground is moist and not frozen.

Do I need permission to cut down a tree?

If the tree has a Tree Preservation Order or you're in a conservation area, you need council consent first.

Can you do regular maintenance visits?

Yes. Weekly, fortnightly or monthly visits can be arranged through the growing season.
