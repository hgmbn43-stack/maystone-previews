Common questions

How long before I can paint new plaster?

Usually 3 to 7 days for a skim, longer for a full re-plaster. It goes from dark to light pink and even in colour when it's dry.

Can you plaster over Artex?

Yes. Most Artex can be skimmed over after preparation. Older Artex may need testing for asbestos first.

Do I need to empty the room?

It helps to clear as much as you can. Anything left is covered and the floor is protected.

Skim or re-plaster?

If the existing plaster is sound, a skim is quicker and cheaper. If it's loose or hollow, it needs re-plastering.
