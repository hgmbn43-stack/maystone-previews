Good to know

Common questions

How long does a new roof last?

A well-fitted tiled or slate roof can last 50 years or more. Flat roofs in EPDM or GRP typically last 20 to 30 years.

Is a leak always the roof?

Not always. Leaks can come from flashing, chimneys, gutters or condensation. An inspection finds the real cause.
