Good to know

Common questions

How often should my boiler be serviced?

Once a year. Most manufacturers need a yearly service to keep the warranty valid, and it catches faults early.

Do you need to be Gas Safe registered to work on a boiler?

Yes. By law, anyone working on gas appliances in the UK must be on the Gas Safe Register. You can check any engineer's number on the Gas Safe website.

Can you do landlord gas safety certificates?

Landlords need a gas safety check every 12 months by a Gas Safe registered engineer. Ask us when you call and we'll book it in.

What should I do if I smell gas?

Call the National Gas Emergency number on 0800 111 999 straight away, open windows and don't use switches or flames.
