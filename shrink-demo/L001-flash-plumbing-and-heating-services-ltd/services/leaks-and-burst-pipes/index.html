Good to know

Common questions

What should I do if I smell gas?

Call the National Gas Emergency number on 0800 111 999 straight away, open windows and don't use switches or flames.

How often should my boiler be serviced?

Once a year. Most manufacturers need a yearly service to keep the warranty valid, and it catches faults early.
